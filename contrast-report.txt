--- Why --accent was the real defect -------------------------------------

The accent is the design's one loud element. In dark it carries 6.55:1; in
light it carried 2.76:1 on cards — quieter than the body text it was meant to
outrank. Stat values, the "Generate smart practice" button and the active tier
filter all read as muted. #665006 restores the relationship at 6.43:1, which
is within 0.12 of the dark theme's figure.


--- Why dark --sub still says FAIL ---------------------------------------

#646669 on #323437 is monkeytype's own "serika dark" pairing, and the redesign
is a deliberate homage to it. Its 2.17:1 is a design choice inherited from the
reference, not a regression introduced here, so it was left alone.

The light theme's --sub was raised anyway (2.34 -> 5.15) because the same ratio
that reads as "muted" on a dark surface reads as "washed out" on light grey.
The two themes therefore diverge on this token on purpose.

If dark --sub should match, #9fa2a7 clears AA on both dark surfaces
(4.88:1 on --bg, 5.32:1 on --sub-alt). That is a large jump from #646669 and
visibly departs from the monkeytype look, so it is worth a deliberate decision
rather than a silent change.
